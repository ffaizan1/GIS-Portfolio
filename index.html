About

GIS work grounded in usability, accuracy, and real-world context.

My work spans environmental justice, community research, accessibility, web GIS, remote sensing, cartography, and geospatial data quality. I enjoy turning technical spatial data into tools that people can actually explore and use.

I have hands-on experience developing and maintaining ArcGIS Online content, Experience Builder applications, StoryMaps, dashboards, web maps, and geospatial datasets. My professional experience includes environmental advocacy with Bayou City Waterkeeper, community-focused GIS research with Friends of the Chicago River, and GIS research support at Northeastern Illinois University.

I am currently completing a Master of Science in Geography & Environmental Studies at Northeastern Illinois University and hold a graduate-level GIS Certificate with a 4.0 GPA.
